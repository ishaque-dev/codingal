Mini Biography: Albert Einstein

Albert Einstein was a theoretical physicist, philosopher of science, and one of the most influential scientists of all time.

Early Life

He was born in Ulm, Germany, and grew up with a deep fascination for mathematics and a curiosity about how the universe works.

Important Achievement

He developed the theory of relativity, which completely changed how we understand space and time.

He is remembered as a true scientific genius whose ideas laid the foundation for modern physics.

One value that made him special was his boundless imagination.

Correction Corner

He was a bad student who failed math in school. He was actually a brilliant student who mastered complex calculus by the age of 15!

Interesting Examples

He is best known for his world-famous equation: E = mc2.

He also proved that light is made of tiny particles, showing that science is full of surprises.

Words That Inspire

He once said, Imagination is more important than knowledge.

A person who never made a mistake never tried anything new.

My Reflection

I admire Albert Einstein because he showed that asking questions and thinking creatively can change the world.
